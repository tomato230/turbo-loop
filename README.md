# Turbo Loop

A top-down arcade racer that runs in the browser. No install, no account.

**Play:** https://tomato230.github.io/turbo-loop/

- Race 3 AI cars over 3 laps on 4 tracks: Green Loop, Desert Dunes, Snow Peak, Night City
- Customize your car in the Garage (name, color, style, stripe) and unlock rare ones with coins
- Race a friend online: create a room, send the link, optionally bet coins (virtual only)

Controls: ↑/W gas · ↓/S brake · ←/→ or A/D steer · Space drift · P pause · M sound · Q menu
